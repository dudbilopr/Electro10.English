science Content Objectives (Physics)
  • check_circle Explain how atomic charge imbalances (`+` vs `-`) arise from electron gain or loss.
  • check_circle Demonstrate Coulomb's Law rule: Opposites attract and like charges repel.
  • check_circle Trace electric field vector directions away from $+q$ and toward $-q$.
translate Language Objectives (English)
  • check_circle Acquire and pronounce academic nouns: proton, electron, neutron, field, charge.
  • check_circle Master dynamic physics verbs: attract, repel, steal, govern, flow.

⏱ Phase 5 • 1 Minute Synthesis

Why Electric Charges & Fields Power Our World

Today, we bridged atomic structure with macroscopic phenomena. Electric charges (`+` & `-`) and invisible electric fields aren't just textbook formulas—they are the physical backbone of modern civilization.

🔌

Electrical Grids & Conductors

Voltage potential creates an electric field inside metallic wires, dictating how electrons flow continuously to power cities, computers, and medical devices.

⚡

Atmospheric High-Voltage Sparks

When cloud separation builds extreme electrostatic charge exceeding air dielectric breakdown ($3 \times 10^6\ \text{V/m}$), lightning forms as a massive neutralizing return stroke.
